03 Author & Purpose

Beauty Requires Creation · Tianbing Zhao

Writing technology, writing philosophy. From software architecture, AI commercial dynamics to Wittgenstein's final words; from cognitive learning methods in the AI era to the nine stages of inner cultivation.

“Beauty Requires Creation” is the site's author name, and the mind behind 9 full-length audiobooks (4 core mental works + 5 era trust observations). The author believes true human progress starts with upgrading one's inner operating system—not infinitely inflating external desires.

04 What is Cognitive Energy Engineering?

The subtitle of this website originates from a systematic experiment on modern mental health: “Inner Civilization: Cognitive Energy Engineering”.

The external world has surged into the information explosion era, yet our inner operating system still runs on ancient versions: fragmented attention, drained emotions, and hollow exhaustion after endless scrolling. This project treats cognition as an understandable, optimizable system, guarding and strengthening personal inner order amid the algorithmic flood.
